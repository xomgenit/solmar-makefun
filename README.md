**SOLMAR — Streets of the Sun**: a complete 2D top-down open-world crime game set in Solmar Bay, an original sun-soaked city. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's Make GTA 6 bounty ($1,666 in USDC).

You are Danny "Dice" Marlow, new in town. Fixer Vera Santos has five jobs for you — steal wheels, run a pickup from the Docks, a 90-second hot delivery to the Pier, payback against the Harbor Kings, and the Solmar Score itself: crack the bank vault and lose a 3-star wanted level.

## What's in the game

- **A city to explore on foot and by car** — a seeded procedural grid with named landmarks (Vera's Diner, the Docks, the Pier, the Bank, Ray's Garage, the Sunset Auto Spa, SBPD HQ, Solmar General), six districts, beach and sea
- **Traffic and pedestrians** — lane-following traffic, parked cars, peds who stroll, flee and get knocked down
- **Cars to steal and drive** — 5 models plus the SBPD cruiser, arcade handling with drifting, handbrake and damage
- **A wanted level (0–5) and police who respond** — foot cops who chase and shoot, cruisers that pursue and ram, busted/wasted outcomes, heat that cools when you hide, and a $150 respray that clears it
- **Missions and a story** — 5 jobs with banners, briefings, markers, a timed delivery, a vault-cracking channel and an escape; free roam after the score
- **Combat** — mouse-aim hitscan shooting with tracers, enemy squads with health bars
- **A full HUD** — health, cash, wanted stars, objective panel, speedometer, round minimap with blips, zone names
- **Title screen, pause menu and saves** — localStorage saves that store the city seed, so CONTINUE rebuilds the same city
- **Polish** — synthesised WebAudio sound (engine, sirens, gunshots, jingles), particles, skid marks, screen shake, day-lit bright art direction

Everything is original: own city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sounds. Fully offline — no network requests, no external assets.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Keyboard + mouse: WASD move/drive, E interact, mouse aim + click to shoot, Shift sprint, Space handbrake, Esc pause, M mute.
